How it works

In. Said. Gone.

From nothing to a private room in 12 seconds. No friction, no identity, no trace.

  1. Arrive

    Open Moment. No sign-up. No cookies. The first page is the room.

  2. Set the clock

    Pick a timer that fits the conversation. The room dissolves when time is up.

  3. Invite anyone

    Share the link. No logins or phone numbers. Names vanish with the room.

  4. Speak freely

    Text, voice, or video inside the room. No recordings.

  5. Let it dissolve

    When time runs out, the room disappears. No files. No cloud.
