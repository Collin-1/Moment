About Moment

Privacy that ends when the room ends.

Moment rejects the modern archive. We created a space where people can speak honestly and leave without a trail.

Designed for trust, not storage.
Why we exist

Conversations deserve a closing ritual.

Most platforms keep logs by default. Moment treats the end of a conversation as sacred. The room closes, the memory ends, and no permanent record remains.

Ephemeral by design

Nothing is stored or indexed. The room is the only place the conversation exists.

Shared control

Everyone can call a vote to close or extend. The room ends together.

Frictionless access

No accounts, no logins, no identity layer. Just a link and a timer.

Principles

Built with accountability and restraint.

No cloud archives

We do not store conversation data or metadata once the room ends.

Explicit consent

Everyone agrees to the timer and the exit. Privacy is the default.

Presence over permanence

Moment exists for the live exchange, not for later retrieval.
